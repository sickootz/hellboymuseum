B.P.R.D. — Fez-o-rama Fez No. 50/50

Fez-o-rama’s B.P.R.D. fez combines dark velvet with a two-tone embroidered bureau insignia. A laser-engraved Mike Mignola signature completes the design, while the museum’s example closes the fifty-piece numbered edition.
